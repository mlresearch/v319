title: 'BanglaNLI: A Benchmark Dataset for Bangla Natural Language Inference'
abstract: We present BanglaNLI, a high-quality Bangla Natural Language Inference dataset
  with expert annotations. The dataset was constructed from 4,200 image captions from
  the BanglaLekha-ImageCaption dataset written by native Bangla speakers, with three
  hypotheses (entailment, contradiction, neutral) generated per premise, yielding
  12,600 carefully annotated sentence pairs. Annotation artefacts were minimised by
  instructing annotators to avoid simple heuristics. Inter-annotator agreement measured
  by Cohen’s Kappa reached $\geq 0.88$, confirming high-quality annotations for this
  under-resourced language with over 200 million native speakers.
