title: 'Towards Trustworthy Email Phishing Detection: Integrating Multi-Modal Deep
  Learning, Federated Learning, and Explainable AI'
abstract: This research presents a robust phishing detection system integrating multi-modal
  deep learning with federated learning and explainable AI. The model combines email
  text analysis with URL structural features using embedding, LSTM, and feature fusion
  layers. In the centralised setup, the model achieved accuracy of 99.6%, precision
  of 99.8%, recall of 99.5%, F1-score of 99.72%, and AUC of 99.85%. The federated
  model maintained competitive performance while protecting user privacy. LIME and
  perturbation analysis reveal which word-level features drive phishing classification
  decisions. Federated learning offers a strong privacy-preserving alternative despite
  marginally lower metrics than centralised training.
