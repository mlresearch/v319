title: Advanced Machine Learning Models for Network Traffic Prediction and Management
abstract: This study employs Random Forest, LSTM, and Support Vector Regression (SVR)
  to forecast network traffic volume at a university. Wireshark was used to capture
  a full month of campus network traffic. Three models were trained on real-world
  traffic data and evaluated across short-term (hourly), medium-term (daily), and
  long-term (weekly) prediction horizons. Across all horizons, LSTM consistently achieved
  lower RMSE and MAE than Random Forest and SVR, demonstrating its suitability for
  capturing temporal dependencies in network traffic prediction.
