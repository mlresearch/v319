title: 'HistoEffiCrossFormer: Cross-Attention CNN–Transformer Fusion with Multi-Scale
  Tokens for Ovarian Cancer Histopathology Classification'
abstract: We propose HistoEffiCrossFormer, a lightweight CNN–Transformer hybrid for
  five-class classification of ovarian cancer histopathology images. The pipeline
  uses Macenko-inspired stain normalisation, EfficientNet-B0 feature extraction, squeeze-and-excitation
  channel attention, multi-scale tokenisation, a 2-layer transformer encoder, and
  cross-attention fusion. Benchmarked against SqueezeNet, ShuffleNetV2, AlexNet, and
  Xception, HistoEffiCrossFormer achieves 0.8267 test accuracy with AUC 0.974, outperforming
  lightweight CNN baselines (0.7067–0.7867 accuracy) and closely matching Xception’s
  AUC (0.975), motivating further validation in external cohorts and whole-slide pipelines.
