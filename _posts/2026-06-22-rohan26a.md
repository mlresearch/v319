title: 'Anchor-Guided Repair: A Defense Mechanism for Enhancing Stability of Compromised
  Pretrained Language Models Against Low-Precision and Weight Noise Attacks'
abstract: We propose Anchor-Guided Repair, a defense mechanism for stabilising large
  language models (LLMs) compromised by weight noise injection and low-precision quantisation
  attacks. The method retrains the attacked model on clean text with an anchor regularisation
  loss that penalises large parameter deviations from a clean reference model. The
  combined objective balances language modelling loss and anchoring regularisation.
  Tested across various quantisation levels and weighted Gaussian noise attack scenarios,
  Anchor-Guided Repair consistently improves stability and performance relative to
  attacked models, demonstrating that anchoring can recover reliability even without
  proprietary training data.
